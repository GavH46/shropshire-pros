METHODOLOGY: Grades are primarily based on real Average Draft Position (ADP) data from Fantasy Football Calculator, compared against the pick number each player was actually taken at — later than ADP suggests = value, earlier = a reach. Grades and records reflect draft capital only — they don't account for injuries, in-season performance, trades, or lineup decisions. Treat this as a fun starting point, not a real prediction.
